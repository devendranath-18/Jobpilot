# Resume Bullet Tailoring — Best Practices

When rewriting resume bullets to match a job description:
- Lead every bullet with a strong action verb (Built, Led, Reduced, Automated, Designed, Optimized)
- Mirror exact JD terminology ONLY for tools/skills the candidate's original bullet ALREADY demonstrates
- Keep each bullet to one line, under 25 words
- Prioritize quantified impact (%, time saved, scale, number of users) over generic responsibilities
- Avoid filler phrases: "responsible for," "helped with," "worked on," "assisted in"
- Preserve specific technical details from the original (frameworks, tools, scale) — don't genericize them away

## CRITICAL RULE — DO NOT VIOLATE
The "missing_skills" list represents skills the candidate does NOT have evidence for.
NEVER insert a missing_skill into a bullet, even with words like "leveraging," "utilizing," "with a focus on," or "including."
Example of a FORBIDDEN edit:
  Original: "Built automation workflows on Workato Otto."
  WRONG:    "Built automation workflows on Workato Otto, leveraging PyTorch and Hugging Face models."
  (PyTorch/Hugging Face were NOT in the original bullet — this is fabrication, even though the sentence reads smoothly.)
Only rewrite using skills, tools, and technologies that are explicitly present in the ORIGINAL bullet text you were given. If a missing_skill isn't in the original bullet, do not mention it anywhere in the tailored bullet, under any phrasing.
