# Cover Letter Writing — Best Practices

## Structure (required, in this exact order)
1. Sender's name and contact info (top of letter — pull from resume: candidate name, and email/phone if present in the resume text)
2. Date (use today's date)
3. Recipient block: company name (use "Hiring Manager" if no specific person is named, and the company name from the JD if available)
4. Subject line: "Re: Application for [Role Title] Position"
5. Salutation ("Dear Hiring Manager," or a named contact if available)
6. Body (3-4 paragraphs, see guidelines below)
7. Closing ("Sincerely," followed by the candidate's name)

## Body content guidelines
- Open with something specific to the role or company — never "I am writing to express my interest"
- Reference 2-3 concrete experiences from the resume that map directly to the JD's stated requirements
- Keep the body between 250-350 words (not counting the header/footer elements)
- Use confident, professional tone — avoid overly casual language and avoid overselling/exaggerating
- Address skill gaps honestly if relevant, framed as eagerness to learn, not as an apology
- End with a clear, brief call to action (e.g. requesting a conversation)
